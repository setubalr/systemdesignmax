# Estilo: Dramatic (`dramatic`)

> High-contrast, theatrical design with bold layouts, immersive visuals, and unconventional compositions that command attention.

- **Família:** narrative-conversion
- **Usar em:** eventos, filmes e campanhas.
- **Evitar em:** apps de trabalho.

## Marca

Dramatic design style is a trend characterized by high-contrast visuals, bold, unconventional layouts, and immersive, theatrical experiences designed to grab user attention.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** modern, clean, high-contrast
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Outfit · display=Outfit · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 400, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

Papéis de cor declarados: primary, neutral, success, warning, danger.

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels
