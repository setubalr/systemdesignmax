# Estilo: Lingo (`lingo`)

> Playful, minimal design with bright colors, rounded shapes, tactile 3D borders, and friendly illustrations for approachable interfaces.

- **Família:** playful-friendly
- **Usar em:** produtos approacháveis (estilo Duolingo).
- **Evitar em:** corporativo sério.

## Marca

Lingo is a duolingo inspired design style that combines minimal layouts with bright colors, rounded shapes, and friendly illustrations to create an engaging and approachable interface. It focuses on clarity and simplicity while adding personality that makes the product feel welcoming and interactive.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** bold, playful
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Nunito · display=Nunito · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32
- Shadows: tactile 3D-like bottom borders (e.g., border-b-4) or soft drop shadows for interactive elements

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid decorative motion without purpose
- avoid ambiguous labels
- avoid mixing multiple visual metaphors
- avoid inaccessible hit areas
