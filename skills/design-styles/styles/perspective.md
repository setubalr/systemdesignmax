# Estilo: Perspective Design (`perspective`)

> Spatial depth design with isometric views, vanishing points, and layered elements that guide attention through 3D-like realism.

- **Família:** tech-futurist
- **Usar em:** produtos técnicos e infográficos.
- **Evitar em:** leitura simples.

## Marca

This style moves beyond flat design by employing techniques like isometric views, vanishing points, and layered elements to guide the user's attention and create a sense of realism or "spatial" depth.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** modern, clean, high-contrast
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Poppins · display=Oswald · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32
