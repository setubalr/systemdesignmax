# Estilo: Corporate (`corporate`)

> Professional, brand-aligned design with structured grids, minimalist layouts, and consistent enterprise patterns.

- **Família:** corporate-data
- **Usar em:** sites institucionais e B2B.
- **Evitar em:** projetos lúdicos.

## Marca

Corporate UI/UX design focuses on professionalism, consistency, and brand alignment, typically featuring minimalist layouts, structured grids, and, in 2026, AI-driven personalization

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** enterprise, premium
- **Escala tipográfica:** desktop-first expressive scale
- **Fontes:** primary=Open Sans · display=Poppins · mono=IBM Plex Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 8pt baseline grid

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit
- design for empty/loading/error states
- ensure responsive behavior by default
- document accessibility rationale

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid decorative motion without purpose
- avoid ambiguous labels
- avoid mixing multiple visual metaphors
- avoid inaccessible hit areas

## Acessibilidade

WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-first interactions, visible focus states, semantic HTML before ARIA, screen-reader tested labels, reduced-motion support, 44px+ touch targets, high-contrast support

## Tom de escrita

concise, confident, helpful, clear, professional, action-oriented, low-jargon
