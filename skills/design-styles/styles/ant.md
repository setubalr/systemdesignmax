# Estilo: Ant (`ant`)

> Structured, enterprise-focused design system emphasizing clarity, consistency, and efficiency for data-dense web applications.

- **Família:** corporate-data
- **Usar em:** ERPs, admins, tabelas e back-offices.
- **Evitar em:** landing pages ou marcas expressivas.

## Marca

Ant Design follows a structured, enterprise-focused design system that emphasizes clarity, consistency, and efficiency in complex web applications.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** data-dense, enterprise
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Plus Jakarta Sans · display=Plus Jakarta Sans · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

Papéis de cor declarados: primary, neutral, success, warning, danger.

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit
- design for empty/loading/error states
- ensure responsive behavior by default
- document accessibility rationale

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid decorative motion without purpose
- avoid ambiguous labels
- avoid mixing multiple visual metaphors
- avoid inaccessible hit areas

## Tom de escrita

concise, confident, helpful, clear, friendly, professional, action-oriented, low-jargon
