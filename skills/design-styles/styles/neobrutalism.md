# Estilo: Neobrutalism (`neobrutalism`)

> Modern take on brutalism with bold borders, vivid accent colors, and raw, high-contrast layouts on warm surfaces.

- **Família:** brutalist-bold
- **Usar em:** marcas ousadas atuais.
- **Evitar em:** contextos conservadores.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** modern, clean, high-contrast
- **Escala tipográfica:** 13/15/17/21/27/35
- **Fontes:** primary=Inter · display=Inter · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Tokens de cor

| Token | Valor |
|---|---|
| primary | `#FDC800` |
| secondary | `#432DD7` |
| success | `#16A34A` |
| warning | `#D97706` |
| danger | `#DC2626` |

Papéis de cor declarados: primary, neutral, success, warning, danger.

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels
