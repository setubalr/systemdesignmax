# Estilo: Skeumorphism (`skeumorphism`)

> Real-world mimicry with textured surfaces, 3D effects, and familiar physical metaphors for intuitive digital interfaces.

- **Família:** retro-game
- **Usar em:** apps para leigos e nostalgia pré-2013.
- **Evitar em:** estéticas flat modernas.

## Marca

a UI/UX design approach that mimics real-world textures, materials, and 3D functionality to make digital interfaces intuitive, familiar, and relatable

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** playful
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Roboto · display=Germania One · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels
