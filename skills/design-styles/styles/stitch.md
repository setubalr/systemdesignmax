# Estilo: Stitch (`stitch`)

> Clean, high-contrast enterprise design for data-driven workflows with intuitive drag-and-drop patterns and structured layouts.

- **Família:** corporate-data
- **Usar em:** ferramentas de trabalho com dados.
- **Evitar em:** marketing.

## Marca

Everything you need – data, apps, and AI in an intuitive drag and drop interface to automate your workflows.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** clean, high-contrast, enterprise
- **Escala tipográfica:** desktop-first expressive scale
- **Fontes:** primary=Ubuntu · display=Oswald · mono=Ubuntu Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** comfortable density mode
- patterns
- images

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid decorative motion without purpose
- avoid ambiguous labels
- avoid mixing multiple visual metaphors
- avoid inaccessible hit areas

confident, helpful, friendly, professional
