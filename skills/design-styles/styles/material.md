# Estilo: Material (`material`)

> Google's Material Design with layered surfaces, dynamic theming, built-in motion, and responsive cross-platform patterns.

- **Família:** corporate-data
- **Usar em:** apps do ecossistema Google/Android.
- **Evitar em:** identidades autorais fortes.

## Marca

UX (user experience) designers use it to create visually consistent, intuitive, and responsive digital experiences. With built-in motion, layering, and dynamic theming, Material Design helps teams deliver accessible, high-quality UIs (user interfaces) across devices and platforms.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** modern, minimal, clean
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Inter · display=Roboto · mono=Fira Code
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels
