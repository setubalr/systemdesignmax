# Estilo: Friendly (`friendly`)

> Approachable, intuitive design with rounded elements, ample whitespace, and soft pastel color palettes.

- **Família:** playful-friendly
- **Usar em:** apps de consumidor e educação.
- **Evitar em:** contextos técnicos densos.

## Marca

Friendly UI/UX design focuses on creating approachable, intuitive, and engaging experiences through, rounded elements, ample whitespace, and, soft, pastel, color, palettes

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** bold, playful, premium
- **Escala tipográfica:** 14/16/18/24/32/40
- **Fontes:** primary=Noto Serif Display · display=Noto Serif Display · mono=Space Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** compact density mode

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels
