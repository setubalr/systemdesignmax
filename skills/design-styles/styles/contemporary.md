# Estilo: Contemporary (`contemporary`)

> Current-era minimalist design with bento grids, dark mode support, and high-performance accessible layouts.

- **Família:** premium-dark
- **Usar em:** produtos tech atuais.
- **Evitar em:** identidades editoriais clássicas.

## Marca

Contemporary UI/UX design focuses on, minimalist, accessible, and high-performance, featuring Bento grids, dark mode, and, AI-powered personalization

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** modern, minimal, bold, playful
- **Escala tipográfica:** desktop-first expressive scale
- **Fontes:** primary=Jost · display=Jost · mono=Overpass Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** comfortable density mode

Papéis de cor declarados: primary, secondary, neutral.

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels

## Acessibilidade

WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-first interactions, visible focus states, semantic HTML before ARIA, screen-reader tested labels, reduced-motion support, 44px+ touch targets, high-contrast support

## Tom de escrita

concise, confident, helpful, friendly, professional
