# Estilo: Cosmic (`cosmic`)

> Futuristic sci-fi aesthetic with dark themes, vibrant neon accents, and immersive spatial elements.

- **Família:** tech-futurist
- **Usar em:** tech, IA e gaming.
- **Evitar em:** marcas humanas e acolhedoras.

## Marca

Cosmic UI/UX design is a futuristic, sci-fi-inspired style characterized by dark themes, vibrant neon accents, and immersive, spatial elements

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** playful, premium
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Audiowide · display=Audiowide · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 400
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels
