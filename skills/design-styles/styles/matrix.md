# Estilo: Matrix (`matrix`)

> A cyber-slick, dark-only Matrix-inspired interface defined by minimalist fashion, high-tech digital elements

- **Família:** tech-futurist
- **Usar em:** tech e projetos temáticos do filme.
- **Evitar em:** clareza corporativa.

## Marca

A cyber-slick, dark-only Matrix-inspired interface defined by minimalist fashion, high-tech digital elements, monospaced typography, and a single deep monochromatic surface. The aesthetic borrows from order-book exchanges and terminal interfaces: ultra-dense layouts, mono numerics, hairline borders, near-square 2px corners, a single brand-green accent that drives every interaction, and a flat dark surface across every section.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** high-contrast
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Space Mono · display=Space Mono · mono=Space Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Regras

**Fazer**
- prefer semantic tokens over raw values
- preserve visual hierarchy
- keep interaction states explicit
- design for empty/loading/error states

**Evitar**
- avoid low contrast text
- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels

concise, confident, helpful, clear, friendly
