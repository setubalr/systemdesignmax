# Estilo: Sketch (`sketch`)

> A friendly, hand-drawn sketch interface inspired by pencil illustrations on warm cream paper. Soft teal brand accents, hand-written display headings, rounded pill controls.

- **Família:** playful-friendly
- **Usar em:** produtos amigáveis em fase de ideação.
- **Evitar em:** corporativo.

## Marca

A friendly, hand-drawn sketch interface inspired by pencil illustrations on warm cream paper. Soft teal brand accents, hand-written display headings, rounded pill controls, dashed card outlines, and chunky offset "pencil-drawn" shadows give every surface a tactile, illustrated feel.

## Fundamentos

- **Estilo visual:** modern, clean, high-contrast
- **Escala tipográfica:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Fontes:** primary=Delicious Handrawn · display=Delicious Handrawn · mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Pesos:** 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
- **Espaçamento:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Regras

**Fazer**

- keep interaction states explicit

**Evitar**

- avoid inconsistent spacing rhythm
- avoid ambiguous labels
